## Smart Farming Assistant

Crop Logic is a web-based Smart Farming Assistant designed to
help people learn about farming, manage farming activities,
ask farming-related questions, and access useful agricultural
tools in one place.

The project was created during my first year as a Computer
Science student at Tshwane University of Technology (TUT).

---

## About Crop Logic

Crop Logic started as a university group project.

Our group consisted of 10 students, and we were given a
farming-related theme and asked to create a website.

At the time, I was still new to programming and was learning
the fundamentals of web development.

Instead of building only a traditional farming information
website, I decided to explore how programming, APIs and
Artificial Intelligence could be used to create a digital
farming assistant.

I researched different technologies and farming-related
problems using online resources, Google and AI tools.

This research led to the idea of Crop Logic.

---

##  What Can Crop Logic Do?

Crop Logic brings several farming-related tools and
features together in one platform.

###  Crop Guidance

Users can learn about:

- How to plant different crops
- Watering requirements
- Suitable soil
- Fertilizers
- Manure
- General crop care

###  AI Farming Assistant

Users can ask an AI farming assistant questions about
agriculture and receive helpful information.

Examples:

> "How often should I water tomatoes?"

> "What type of soil is suitable for this crop?"

> "What fertilizer should I use?"

---

###  Sherwon AI

Sherwon AI acts as an intelligent guide inside Crop Logic.

If users become lost or don't know where to find a particular
feature, Sherwon AI is designed to help guide them through
the website.

Sherwon AI is different from the farming AI:

**Farming AI → answers farming questions**

**Sherwon AI → helps users navigate Crop Logic**

---

### 💬 Discussion Hub

The Discussion Hub allows users to ask farming-related
questions and receive answers from different people.

For example:

> "What is the best way to plant this crop?"

Different users can share their own experiences,
recommendations and opinions.

This allows people to learn from multiple perspectives.

---

### Farming Quiz

Crop Logic includes a farming quiz game designed to make
learning about agriculture more interactive.

The quiz can help younger users and beginners learn about:

- Crops
- Plants
- Soil
- Water
- Fertilizers
- Farming practices
- Agricultural knowledge

---

### Weather Station

Crop Logic integrates weather information to help users
understand weather conditions that may affect their farming
activities.

The weather functionality uses the OpenWeather API.

---

###  Farming Calendar

Users can use the farming calendar to plan activities such
as:

- Planting
- Watering
- Fertilizing
- Checking crops
- Pest control
- Other farming tasks

The goal is to help users organize their farming activities.

---

### Fertilizer Calculator

Crop Logic includes a fertilizer calculator that helps users
estimate the amount of fertilizer required for their farming
needs.

---

### Budget-Based Fertilizer Assistance

Crop Logic also explores helping users find fertilizer
options that fit within their available budget.

The idea is to help users answer:

> "What suitable fertilizer can I afford?"

---

###  Farming & Pest Tips

Users can access farming tips and information about dealing
with common crop and pest problems.
